Pinterest dark mode that's easy on the eyes

A soft cement-gray dark theme (never pure black) and a warm sepia theme for Pinterest. One click, no reload, and your pins keep their original colors.

Three themes

Dark, sepia or light: you pick

Dark

A soft cement-gray background (#2b2d2f) with comfortable contrast for text and icons. Not pure black, so it doesn't glare at night.

Sepia

A warm paper tone (#f4ecd8) with brown text, made for long browsing sessions.

Light

Pinterest exactly as it comes. Switch back with one click.

Built for Pinterest

The page changes. Your pins don't.

Photos and videos keep their original colors. The extension also works on the other Pinterest sites and regional domains, switches themes instantly in open tabs, and speaks 55 languages automatically, following your browser.

Install

Install the Pinterest dark mode extension

  1. Download the .zip and unzip it into a folder you'll keep. manifest.json must be directly inside it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose that folder.
  4. Pin the extension, click its icon and pick Dark or Sepia. Refresh any Pinterest tab that was already open.

Works in Chrome and Chromium-based browsers (confirmed in Brave). Full guide and updates: README on GitHub.

FAQ

Questions about Pinterest dark mode

How do I get dark mode on Pinterest in Chrome?

Install this extension, click its toolbar icon and choose Dark. The page turns a soft cement gray instantly, and you can switch to Sepia or back to Light at any time.

Why cement gray instead of black?

Pure black next to bright photos creates harsh contrast that tires the eyes. A dark gray keeps the page calm while the pins stay vivid.

Is it free? Does it collect data?

It's free. It collects nothing: your choice is stored with Chrome's own sync storage, with no analytics, ads or remote code. Read the privacy policy.

Does it work on other websites?

Yes, optionally. Turn on "Apply to other sites" in the popup and your browser asks for permission first. Leave it off and the extension only touches Pinterest.

Is it on the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. Until then, install it from the .zip as shown above.
