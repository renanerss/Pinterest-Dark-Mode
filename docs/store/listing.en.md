# Chrome Web Store listing — English (default)

## Name

Soft Themes for Pinterest

## Short description

Dark mode (soft cement gray) and sepia themes for Pinterest, optional on other sites. Not affiliated with Pinterest.

## Description

Pinterest is bright. This extension adds a real dark mode and a sepia theme to Pinterest, both easier on the eyes, switchable in one click from the toolbar:

• Dark — a soft cement-gray background (not pure black), with comfortable contrast for text and icons.
• Sepia — a warm paper tone for long browsing sessions.
• Light — Pinterest as it comes, one click away.

Your pins stay exactly as they were posted: the themes change the page, never the photos.

How it works
• Pick a theme from the toolbar icon. Open tabs update immediately, no reload needed.
• Pinterest works out of the box on its main domains.
• Want the same look elsewhere? Turn on "Apply to other sites". Chrome will ask for permission first; the extension asks for nothing else, and you can turn it off at any time.
• Available in 55 languages; it follows your browser's language automatically.

Privacy
The extension collects no data. Your chosen theme is stored with chrome.storage.sync (it syncs through your own Google account) and never sent anywhere else. A tiny cache of the last theme is kept in the visited site's own local storage so the theme can load without a flash. No analytics, no ads, no remote code.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Pinterest. Pinterest is a trademark of Pinterest, Inc.
